# NO Map Overhaul v0.6.0 beta 4

Teammates on the same Steam team can now see each other's map drawings. Sharing is off until you turn it on, and it's a beta, so please open an issue with your BepInEx log if a drawing doesn't show up.

Beta 4 is mostly about performance. Some players saw their frame rate sink during long multiplayer sessions with several teammates sharing drawings. I couldn't reproduce that on my own PCs, even with four players sharing heavily, so this beta makes drawing much cheaper and turns on logging that should pin it down. If your game slows down in a session, please save `BepInEx/LogOutput.log` before restarting the game and send it to me.

## New in beta 4

- **Custom colours.** The Tools menu's swatches get a second row for up to six colours of your own. Press **+** to pick one on a colour wheel with a brightness slider, then **Add**. Right-click a custom swatch to remove it. They're kept between games.
- **Other channels' dimming.** F1 → Map Tools / Drawing → **Other channels opacity** and **Other channels brightness** set how faint drawings on other channels show. The defaults keep the old look.
- **Logging is on.** Log performance and Log network are on in this beta, even if you turned them off before; turn either off and it stays off. Every 5 seconds the log records the frame rate, what the mod spent and on what, Unity's UI and render costs, and each teammate's sharing traffic, which is what I need to find the slowdown.

## Faster in beta 4

- The circles' rings in the 3D view are drawn by a shader on your graphics card instead of being rebuilt every frame. If they look wrong on your card, F1 → Diagnostics → **Draw 3D rings on the CPU** switches back.
- Text notes on the map draw from a few shared meshes instead of two objects each, map labels are placed much faster, and drawings off the edge of the map cost nothing.
- Drawings on units redraw only when a unit moves, and teammates' unchanged drawings are kept instead of rebuilt twice a second. With three teammates drawing heavily, the mod's memory churn fell by about 85%, so the game stops for garbage collection far less often.

## Fixed in beta 4

- A drawing error no longer switches the whole mod off. The mod rebuilds its graphics after a short pause and your drawings, teammates' drawings and sharing carry on. Beta 3 could switch itself off this way.
- Freezes of up to 1.5 seconds when sharing first connected.
- Map labels on moving units keep their places instead of hopping around. A circle in front of you always shows its 3D ring.

While building sharing, I described how it works to Shockfront: off by default, same team only, only the drawings you make, sent over Steam's own networking. Their dev team reviewed it and said it's fine, and I waited for that before making the code public.

## New

- **Channels.** On the full map, open Tools → Channels, type your Broadcast channels and any Receive only channels (up to ten each, comma separated), turn sharing on, and press Apply. You see a teammate's drawing when they broadcast a channel you broadcast or receive.
- **Draw to.** The tool strip shows which channel new drawings go to. Click a numbered Broadcast button or press Shift+1 through Shift+0 to switch.
- **What gets shared.** Pen strokes, circles, text notes, waypoint routes, and Bearing/range (BRA) arrows. A drawing on a unit sends only which unit it's on, and each player places it from their own tracks, so a BRA arrow is measured from their own picture. When their side loses the unit, the drawing stops at its last known position and reads `lost`.
- **Erasing a teammate's drawing.** If you both broadcast its channel, the eraser or a right click asks its owner's game to remove it. Receive only channels are read-only.
- **Dimming.** Drawings on a channel other than your Draw to channel show fainter and darker, yours and teammates' alike. The Waypoint tool's guide line dims with its route.
- **Log network** (F1 → Diagnostics) records pairing, snapshots and rejections with anonymous peer labels. See [logging multiplayer sharing](../USER_GUIDE.md#logging-multiplayer-sharing).

## Fixed in beta 3

- Circles, text notes and waypoint routes attached to a unit now share, the way BRA arrows already did. In beta 2 they stayed on your own map.
- A shared drawing on a unit now stops where your side last knew the unit and reads `lost`, like your own drawings, instead of disappearing. It stays hidden only if your side has never known that unit.
- Quitting the game from a mission with sharing on no longer crashes.
- A teammate's drawings update on your map at most twice a second, and only so many can change at once, so a teammate can't keep your game busy redrawing. Changes over the limit wait and still arrive.
- Changed drawings, yours and teammates', redraw in place instead of being rebuilt.

## Fixed in beta 2

- Teammates who turn sharing on mid-mission now pair. The mod asks a teammate three times per mission (at 0, 60 and 300 seconds) and pairs whenever they answer.
- Paired teammates send a small heartbeat every 5 seconds instead of resending every drawing. Full drawings go out only after an edit, a new pairing, or when a teammate is behind.
- A burst of traffic resets that teammate's channel instead of blocking them for the mission, and a teammate on another protocol version is shown as such.
- Limits are 100 drawings and 2,500 pen points, and routes of up to 50 waypoints. Teammates' drawings share the received space evenly.
- The Waypoint tool keeps following the route you fly when you switch channels. The Text tool refuses notes with nothing visible in them, and a colour setting with zero alpha draws opaque.
- Label plates stop below Unity's mesh vertex limit instead of failing to draw.
- Leaving a mission undoes each of the mod's changes even when one of them fails.

## Limits

- Everyone who shares needs beta 3 or beta 4, which share with each other (protocol 6). Older builds, beta 2 included, are ignored.
- Drawings stay within your faction. Spectators and dedicated-server or UDP sessions get none.
- Channel names are an honour system within the team, not access control.

## Upgrading

Install over 0.5.1 or an earlier beta. Settings carry over. Beta 4 adds a file, `nomapoverhaul-shaders.bundle`, which goes beside `NoMapOverhaul.dll`; both packages include it. Without it the rings are drawn the old way.

## Test status

- 330 automated tests pass.
- Beta 4 was checked in game through automated runs: the shader's rings against the old ones in the same paused frame, the batched notes against the old ones side by side, the custom colours and rail at 1440p and 1080p, and four players sharing heavy drawings for 15 minutes on one PC with no slowdown. The slowdown players reported in bigger sessions hasn't been reproduced, which is what the logging is for.
- A two-PC Steam session in Escalation checked the unit drawings and the quit fix: drawings attached to units shared, and both games quit without crashing. That build came before the update limits and in-place redraws, which have unit tests and a single-player check of undo and redo. A teammate's changing drawings, and shared drawings reading `lost`, haven't been checked between two PCs yet.
- Checked in game on Nuclear Option 0.34.2 with BepInEx 5.4.23.4 across two PCs on Steam: pairing in both join orders, a teammate without the mod as client and as host, opposite teams, reconnects, channel changes, late enablement, sharing off and on, owner and teammate erase, dimming, and received drawings on the map, minimap and HUD.
- Known issue: two of about twelve test sessions saw one client freeze for about a minute shortly after pairing. It hasn't recurred since logging was added. If it happens, send the BepInEx log.
