# NO Map Overhaul v0.6.0

Your flight can now share map drawings. 0.6.0 also gives the runways a new, higher-contrast look that reads at a glance over the map's green.

![A viper route at full strength and a dimmed command circle on the full map, with Draw to: viper on the bar](../images/sharing-dimmed.jpg)

## Share drawings with your flight

Sharing is off until you turn it on. On the full map, press `H`, click **Channels**, type a channel name such as `viper` under **Broadcast**, turn sharing on, and click **Apply**. Teammates on your side who run the mod on the same channel then see your drawings, and you see theirs.

- Pen lines, circles, notes, waypoint routes and Bearing/range arrows all share. Arrows, notes and circles show in the 3D view too.
- It works on player-hosted games and Steam dedicated servers, including the big community ones, and the server needs no mod.
- Point at a teammate's drawing to see who drew it.
- A drawing on a contact uses each player's own picture. When their side loses the contact, it stops where it was last seen and reads `lost`.
- **Receive only** channels let a flight watch the package lead's plan without drawing on it. `Shift+1` to `Shift+0` switch the channel you're drawing to.

The [sharing guide](../SHARING_GUIDE.md) walks through a squadron's setup, erasing a teammate's drawing, and what the status line means.

Before making sharing public, I described it to Shockfront: off by default, your own side only, only the drawings you make, sent over Steam's own networking. Their dev team reviewed it and said it's fine.

## A clearer map

- Runways are black with white numbers in a black outline, with a dashed centre line. Zoom in and each number moves onto the runway, where it's painted.
- Parallel runways read 06L and 06R instead of two 06s, and the numbers match the one ATC calls.
- Airbase names and landing zones show by default, and the HUD callout names the airbase, such as `NBSCLI RWY 27`.
- The dark outline around map text actually draws now. In earlier versions it never showed.

If you're upgrading, your first start of 0.6.0 moves each look setting that's still at its old default to the new one, once. Anything you changed stays as you set it.

## Also new

- Up to six drawing colours of your own, from the **+** under the rail's swatches.
- Drawing is much cheaper, so heavy planning costs less frame rate.

## Upgrading

Nuclear Option Mod Manager updates it for you. By hand, extract the plugin-only archive over your install. It adds `nomapoverhaul-shaders.bundle` beside the DLL. Settings carry over from 0.5.1. If you ran a 0.6.0 beta, 0.6.0 switches its diagnostic logs back off once.

Everyone who shares needs 0.6.0. Servers in UDP mode and spectators can't share, and channel names are an honour system, not passwords.

If sharing misbehaves, turn on **F1 → Diagnostics → Log network** for both players, reproduce it, and [open an issue](https://github.com/baanish/NO-Map-Overhaul/issues) with both players' `BepInEx/LogOutput.log`.

The [changelog](../../CHANGELOG.md) lists every change. 360 automated tests pass, and [testing](../TESTING.md) records the in-game and two-PC checks.
